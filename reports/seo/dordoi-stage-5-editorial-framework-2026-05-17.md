# Dordoi.help Stage 5 Editorial Framework — 2026-05-17

## Content Principles

- Write for real buyers, sellers, and service users, not only for search robots.
- Give practical instructions: what to prepare, what to ask, what to compare, and what to check before payment or delivery.
- Do not promise guarantees of deals, delivery, quality, price, speed, or supplier reliability unless the business can prove and legally support it.
- Do not use fake data, fake reviews, fake ratings, or invented success cases.
- Do not publish vendor phone numbers, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, map links, exact locations, or other gated contact data in articles, HTML, JSON-LD, sitemap, or RSC payload.
- Do not say “we checked all suppliers” or similar claims unless there is a documented verification process.
- Do not publish prices or delivery times as facts without a source or clear “depends on partner/route/order” wording.
- Avoid thin articles. Each guide should solve one clear search intent and include enough practical detail to be useful without keyword stuffing.

## Standard Guide Structure

Each published guide should include:

- SEO title
- meta description
- H1
- short intro / excerpt
- H2/H3 sections
- practical checklist
- internal links to relevant sections
- FAQ block
- safe CTA to catalog, buyer-service, cargo, country pages, or category pages
- BlogPosting schema
- `datePublished` and `dateModified`
- editorial note through careful disclaimers where the topic touches payments, delivery, guarantees, or deal risk

## Internal Linking Rules

Each guide should link to 3-6 relevant internal pages, usually:

- `/catalog`
- one relevant category page
- one relevant country page
- `/buyer-service` or `/kargo-dordoi` when applicable
- `/for-buyers`
- `/faq` or `/how-it-works`

Category guides should link to their category page. Country delivery guides should link to their country page and cargo page. Buyer guides should link to buyer-service, for-buyers, catalog, and FAQ.

## Quality Gate

Before publishing a guide:

- title is unique
- H1 is unique
- slug is stable and human-readable
- content has a single clear intent
- no private vendor contacts or exact locked locations
- no unsupported claims
- no fake reviews or ratings
- FAQ is useful and visible
- at least 3 relevant internal links
- no keyword stuffing
- article appears in `/ru/blog`
- canonical and sitemap include the published URL
- BlogPosting schema is valid and privacy-safe

## Stage 5 Publishing Policy

Stage 5 is RU-first. Localized versions of the new long-form guides should be added only after native-language review or after Google/Yandex data shows which guide clusters deserve localization first.
